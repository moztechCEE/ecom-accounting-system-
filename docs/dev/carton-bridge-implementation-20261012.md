# ERP carton bridge implementation — 2026-10-12

Source: isolated `codex/erp-carton-20261012`, base `5c882235aba555b52efa1e899d01c64b6c616b60`, repository `moztechCEE/ecom-accounting-system-`. No shared DEV or production service was deployed, no database or real business record was accessed, and no migration was added.

## Behavior

- Both carton label variants encode `CARTON:<existing cartonId>` in QR. Code128 and carton identity remain unchanged. SN cartons retain full first/last SN text, including wrapping long manufacturer serials. The normal paper sizes remain 60 × 75 mm with SN and 60 × 45 mm without SN, scaled by carton width. No-SN QR is placed at x37/y8/20 mm within its 45 mm page.
- `serialSource: existing` accepts a pasted list or one text column from TXT/CSV/XLSX. Exact ASCII serials retain case, order and leading zeros; duplicates, whitespace, commas, control characters and carton/draft prefixes are rejected. Numeric/formula spreadsheet cells are not coerced to serials. Quantity must equal the list length; capacity determines full and partial final cartons.
- Existing serial registration uses the original SN values and never reads or advances `sn_label_counters`. Activation keeps the existing global allocation lock, draft revision, product snapshot validation, preview token and replay protection. `sn_label_serials` ownership is checked globally before insertion. Existing and no-SN cartons may leave unknown manufacturing dates empty; generated serials still require that date.
- Product EAN may be null. An empty EAN in SN profile editing explicitly clears that field; omission preserves it. Neither SKU nor carton ID is substituted for EAN. Warranty XLSX remains unavailable without EAN.
- Draft `sku` remains the product identity. `wmsItemCode` is an editable cross-system mapping prefilled from SKU for review, not proof of ECOUNT equivalence. New internal scan codes follow `ITM-[A-Za-z0-9._-]{1,96}`; if SKU cannot provide such a code, it remains empty for manual configuration. Cross-system exported item codes are limited to 100 characters.
- The authenticated, company-scoped `POST /sn-labels/batches/:id/manifest` requires inventory-update access. The export dialog requires explicit item/scan-code review and a complete ordinal range of up to 100 cartons. Historical active batches can supply that reviewed mapping at export; the chosen mapping is audited, while their label/SN contents remain unchanged.
- `corely-cartons-v1` exports `sourceSystem: corely-erp`, `sourceReference: batchId`, and carton lines containing `itemCode`, `productName`, nullable `ean/internalScanCode`, `quantity` and exact `actualSN`. The same batch can export several ordinal ranges. No sealed status or stock-posting flag is asserted. WMS imports drafts and warehouse staff verify/seal them separately.
- These operations write label/carton/audit records only. They do not create `InventorySerialNumber`, ECOUNT receipts, shipments or inventory/accounting movements. Actual ECOUNT deduction stays with the established sales workflow.
